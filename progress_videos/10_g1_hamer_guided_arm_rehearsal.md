# G1 arm rehearsal: provenance

Video: `10_g1_hamer_guided_arm_rehearsal.mp4`.

This is an empty-hand arm rehearsal, not a pickup. HaMeR supplied softened relative hand-orientation changes. The robot-space reach path, posture preferences and timing were designed/tuned. G1 IK solved the shoulder, elbow and wrist. Fingers used the existing open preshape; they were not retargeted from HaMeR.

The displayed motion was recorded from torque-driven MuJoCo simulation. The dart remained free and stationary on the table. Both 0.5 ms and 0.25 ms trials passed the rehearsal checks. See `experiment3/ARM_IK.md` and `experiment3/research/arm_ik/report.json`.
